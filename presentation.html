Challenges

What Made This Hard

Procedural Terrain Generation

No map tiles — we generate a 1024×1024 canvas texture from scratch with six zone bands, contour lines, and vintage styling.

Performance at Scale

7,083 markers + Three.js + D3 on one page. Solved with lazy init, IntersectionObserver, and texture caching.

Three.js Memory Leaks

Switching cuisines leaked GPU resources. Had to carefully dispose geometries and re-bind controls on each change.

Making Data Tell a Story

Designed a narrative arc — hook → assumption → surprise → exploration → insight — across 9 sections.
