Before we go further, let's define exactly what we mean. We built a data-driven formula to identify the best restaurant opportunities.

The Gem Score Formula
Each cuisine is scored on 4 weighted dimensions
Gem Score = 0.3 Low Competition + 0.3 Quality + 0.2 Stability + 0.2 Demand
